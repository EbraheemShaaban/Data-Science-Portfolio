# Healthcare Appointment No-Show Analysis

**Portfolio project | Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn · SciPy · scikit-learn**

## Overview

This project analyzes appointment-level records from public clinics in Vitória, Brazil, to explore patterns associated with missed medical appointments (`NoShow`). It combines data-quality checks, feature engineering, rate-based exploratory data analysis, a confounding-aware comparison of SMS reminders, a chi-square association test, reusable CSV exports, and baseline classification models.

The goal is to demonstrate a reproducible analytics workflow—not to make clinical decisions or claim causal explanations.

## Business questions

- What is the no-show rate in the cleaned analysis dataset?
- How does the rate vary by waiting time, weekday, age group, and recorded patient/appointment characteristics?
- Does the observed SMS/no-show relationship change when appointments are compared within waiting-time groups?
- Is there a statistical association between SMS status and appointment outcome?
- How do Logistic Regression and Random Forest compare on held-out data?

## Dataset

The source is the public medical appointments dataset commonly distributed as `KaggleV2-May-2016.csv` or `Data.csv`, with fields such as patient and appointment identifiers, booking and appointment dates, age, neighbourhood, health-condition flags, SMS reminder status, and the `No-show` outcome. ## What is included

1. **Data quality audit** — missing values, exact duplicate rows, duplicate appointment IDs, invalid ages, invalid dates, and unexpected target values.
2. **Data cleaning** — standardizes column names and dates; excludes records with invalid age/date/target values; retains an audit of cleaning decisions.
3. **Feature engineering** — `WaitDays`, appointment weekday, booking weekday, month, age group, and waiting-time group.
4. **Rate-based EDA** — calculates appointment counts and no-show rates by relevant groups, rather than relying only on raw counts.
5. **Waiting-time and SMS analysis** — compares SMS/no-SMS rates overall and within waiting-time bands to highlight potential confounding.
6. **Statistical testing** — chi-square test and Cramér's V for the association between SMS status and outcome.
7. **Baseline machine learning** — compares Logistic Regression and Random Forest using a held-out test set, class-aware metrics, and a confusion matrix. Patient-grouped splitting is used when patient IDs are available.
8. **Reporting outputs** — exports KPI tables, data-quality logs, subgroup rates, correlations, and model metrics for Excel or Power BI.

## Methodology notes

- `NoShow = 1` means the appointment was missed; `NoShow = 0` means it was attended.
- `WaitDays` is computed from normalized calendar dates. Negative wait times are flagged and excluded from wait-time analysis.
- No-show rates use the number of appointments in each group as the denominator.
- SMS status is observational. A raw difference between SMS groups does not prove that reminders cause more or fewer no-shows; waiting time and other variables may confound the relationship. The chi-square test also does not control for confounders or prove causation.
- Baseline model metrics must be calculated by running the notebook on the source CSV. Do not add performance numbers to a CV before verifying them.
- The model uses `SMSReceived` as a feature because it is available in this dataset. A real use case must define the prediction time and exclude features unavailable at that point.

## Limitations and responsible use

- This is a single observational dataset from a particular place and period; patterns may not generalize to other clinics.
- Statistical association is not causation. Prospective evaluation or an appropriate causal design would be needed to evaluate interventions such as SMS reminders.
- The model is educational and is not a validated clinical tool. It must not be used to deny, delay, or restrict healthcare access.
- Patient identifiers and row-level data should not be published. Check the dataset's terms and provenance before redistributing it.
- A future extension could build a Power BI dashboard from aggregated CSV outputs and validate recommendations with healthcare stakeholders.
